Our Food Guide 🍽️

The ultimate list of the best date night spots, huge flavors, and local classics in Wrocław.

Spicy & Indian 🍛

Mango Mama 🥭

A beautiful, trendy spot with neon lights. Perfect fusion of Indian (Biryani, Butter Chicken) and Asian (Pad Thai) so we can both get what we crave!

India Concept 🫓

A gorgeous, cozy upscale spot in the historic center. Amazing Biryani, fresh garlic naan, and perfect for a romantic date night.

Asian & Noodles 🍜

WOO THAI Restaurant 🍤

The most authentic Thai food in the city with a beautiful, warm interior. Massive portions of amazing shrimp and veggie curries.

The Kebab Fix 🌯

KEBABIXX & MR KEBAB 🥙

When we just need a massive, classic Döner fix! Kebabixx for "craft" fresh-baked bread, or Mr Kebab for giant, perfectly seasoned portions.

Local & Trendy Vibes 🍕

Pierogarnia Stary Młyn 🥟

Right on the Market Square! The absolute best traditional Polish dumplings (Pierogi) with tons of vegetarian cheese and potato options.

Iggy Pizza 🍕

The coolest neon-lit vibe and the best Neapolitan pizza in Wrocław. Always packed with couples, amazing energy.

Basecamp Setup 🛒

Our master plan for fresh Halal meat and ingredients near Sikorskiego 13 for Beef Bhuna and Shrimp Pasta.

Auchan 🏪

The ultimate top-tier hypermarket! For a massive haul, specialty items, and literally everything we could ever want, this is the king.

🛍️ Big grocery run
Carrefour 🛒

The absolute best for a massive selection of fresh goods, amazing snacks, and high-quality ingredients for our cooking nights.

🥐 Premium picks
Safa Meats (Ładna 4/6) 🥩

Our primary stop for strictly Halal beef (Wołowina) and chicken (Kurczak). They also stock extra Asian spices just in case!

🚋 25 min (Tram 10/33)
🚗 10-15 min Uber/Bolt
Biedronka (Braniborska 14) 🧅

The absolute closest option for our base veggies and dairy. We will grab heavy cream, onions, garlic, and pasta here.

🚶 10 min walk
📏 800m away
Lidl (Braniborska 82) 🍤

Located just down the street from Biedronka. The perfect backup spot with a great freezer section for your shrimp (Krewetki).

🚶 15-20 min walk
🚗 3 min Uber/Bolt
